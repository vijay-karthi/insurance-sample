01 / The problem

THE CHEAPEST POLICY
ISN’T ALWAYS THE
BEST PROTECTION.

Insurance plans come with different features, limits and exclusions. When customers focus only on price, they may unknowingly choose limited coverage — and discover the difference only when they need to make a claim.

That’s why we exist.

Customer First Marketing Services was founded to close this gap. We help customers understand what they are buying and how their policy can help them when it matters most.
